The 11 puzzle types of Zirve

Every floor of the tower is one of these games. Four are open from the first floor, and a new one joins at each camp after that. Each page below has the rules, how the puzzle grows as you climb, tips and a real screenshot.

Color match

From floor 1

sliding tile-matching puzzle · Drag

Bring matching tiles together and clear the board.

How to play Color match →

Path draw

From floor 1

one-line path puzzle · Draw

Reach the flag with one line, without lifting your finger.

How to play Path draw →

Block fit

From floor 1

block fitting puzzle · Drag, tap to rotate

Fit every piece into the gaps on the board.

How to play Block fit →

Memory

From floor 1

card matching memory game · Tap

Flip the cards and find the matches.

How to play Memory →

Flow

From floor 11

connect the dots puzzle · Draw

Connect matching ends and fill the board.

How to play Flow →

Pipe turn

From floor 21

pipe rotation puzzle · Tap to rotate

Turn the pipes and get the water flowing.

How to play Pipe turn →

Color sort

From floor 31

color sort puzzle with tubes · Tap

Sort the colours until every tube holds one.

How to play Color sort →

Arrows

From floor 41

arrow escape puzzle · Tap

Clear the arrows in the right order.

How to play Arrows →

Gates

From floor 51

sliding block puzzle · Drag or tap

Lead every block out through the gate of its own colour.

How to play Gates →

Lookouts

From floor 61

Star Battle style placement puzzle · Tap

Place one lookout tower in every row, column and coloured region.

How to play Lookouts →

Lights

From floor 71

light every square puzzle · Tap

Light up every square.

How to play Lights →

See you at the summit.

Zirve is free on the App Store. The first floors are waiting.

Zirve

Climb the tower, every floor a puzzle.

Zirve is a free logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad by mm-apps, an independent developer in Türkiye. Climb a tower where every floor is a short timed puzzle from 11 types.
