From floor 51

Gates: sliding block puzzle

Gates is a sliding block puzzle: blocks move only along their long side, and each one leaves the board through a gate of its own colour. No block can leave on the first move, so the puzzle is deciding which block to park where.

How to play

Lead every block out through the gate of its own colour.

  1. Drag a block: it only slides the way its arrows point. With a clear way it leaves through the matching gate; a tap is enough.
  2. The first move is never an exit: you have to park a block aside to open a way first.

Higher up

  • From a 6×6 board to 8×8, from 7 blocks to 14
  • At least 3, later 4 planned parks on every board
  • From floor 80 a locked block; from floor 100 a 2×2 block that slides any direction

Tips

  • Find a block close to its gate and see what stands in its way. That is usually the block to move first.
  • Park the blocking piece where it does not close another block's lane.
  • A block leaving never blocks anything, so let every block out as soon as its way is clear.
  • Read the line under the board: when a move does not happen it tells you why, such as a locked block, the wrong gate or a closed path.
  • From floor 80 a grey block is locked. It opens only after every block of its key colour has left.

Where it appears in the tower

Gates opens at floor 51, right after camp 50, and that floor is always Gates. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

Sliding block and parking puzzles are an old family; a well-known example is the Chinese Klotski puzzle. Zirve's version differs in that blocks leave through gates of their own colour instead of one shared exit.
