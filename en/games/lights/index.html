From floor 71

Lights: light every square puzzle

Lights is the last game to open and the most mathematical one: tapping a square flips it and its four neighbours, lit to dark and dark to lit. The floor is solved when every square is lit.

How to play

Light up every square.

  1. Tap a square: it and its up, down, left and right neighbours (a + shape) flip. Dark ones light up, lit ones go dark.
  2. Hold your finger down to see which squares will flip; diagonals are not affected.

Higher up

  • From a 3×3 board to 5×5
  • From 3 to 10 taps to solve
  • Order matters: think first, then tap

Tips

  • The order of taps does not matter, and two taps on the same square cancel out. Every square needs at most one tap.
  • Work row by row: tap the square under each dark square of the row above. The dark squares move down until only the bottom row is left.
  • The pattern left in the bottom row tells you which squares of the top row to tap. Tap them and work down again.
  • A tap in a corner changes three squares, on an edge four, in the middle five. Corners are good for small fixes.

Where it appears in the tower

Lights opens at floor 71, right after camp 70, and that floor is always Lights. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

It is a version of the puzzle known as lights out, named after a handheld game from the 1990s. In Zirve the goal is reversed: light every square.
