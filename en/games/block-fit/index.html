From floor 1

Block fit: block fitting puzzle

Block fit is a packing puzzle: drag the pieces from the tray onto the board until every gap is filled. Tap to rotate; higher up, some pieces only fit after you flip them.

How to play

Fit every piece into the gaps on the board.

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board; tap a piece to turn it.
  2. Tap a placed piece to take it back; the floor is done when everything fits.

Higher up

  • From a 6×6 board to 8×8, from 3 pieces to 6
  • From floor 30: mirrored pieces you flip with a long press
  • Bigger pieces, tighter gaps

Tips

  • Start with the largest or oddest piece. Small pieces can fill whatever is left.
  • Fill corners and narrow gaps first: few pieces fit there.
  • Count squares. If a gap has a size no remaining piece can make, something is in the wrong place: tap a placed piece to send it back to the tray.
  • From floor 30, a piece with the ↻ badge will not fit by rotating alone. Press and hold it to flip it.
  • When the line under the board turns red and says undo, a piece already placed is blocking the rest.

Where it appears in the tower

Block fit is one of the four games of the first floors. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

Packing puzzles with pieces made of squares are known as polyomino or pentomino puzzles, and tangram works the same way with triangles. In Zirve the empty space is cut from the pieces themselves, so every board has an exact fit.
