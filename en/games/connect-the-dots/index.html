Flow: connect the dots puzzle

Flow asks you to join each pair of matching ends with a line. Lines cannot cross, every square can hold only one line, and the board is only solved when every square is filled.

How to play

Connect matching ends and fill the board.

  1. Draw a line from a coloured dot to its matching dot.
  2. Lines can't cross and every square must be filled; an unfinished line vanishes when you lift your finger.

Higher up

  • From a 5×5 board to 7×7
  • From 3 colours to 6
  • Blocked squares you have to route around

Tips

  • Start with pairs along the edges and in the corners: their lines have the fewest ways to go.
  • Every square must be filled, so a line that leaves a one-square pocket is wrong. Let lines hug the walls and each other.
  • An empty corner square has only two neighbours, so a line must pass through it along the edge.
  • Draw a line in one go. Lifting your finger before you reach its pair erases it; finished lines stay.
  • Going back over your own line shortens it, and starting again from an end resets that colour.

Where it appears in the tower

Flow opens at floor 11, right after camp 10, and that floor is always Flow. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

These puzzles are often called connect the dots or line puzzles. Some versions only ask you to join the pairs; Zirve's version always asks you to fill the whole board too.
