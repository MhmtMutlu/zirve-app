Arrows: arrow escape puzzle

Arrows fills the board with straight and bent arrows. Tap one and it slides off the board in the direction of its head, but only if the line from its head to the edge is empty; a blocked arrow bounces back and costs time.

How to play

Clear the arrows in the right order.

  1. Tap an arrow: it slides the way its head points, its body follows, and it leaves the board if the way is clear.
  2. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps back and costs 3 seconds; clear the ones in front first.

Higher up

  • From a 7×7 board to 11×11, up to 85% full
  • Short arrows next to winding snakes up to 12 cells long
  • Longer "this one first" chains

Tips

  • Look from the arrowhead to the edge of the board. If nothing is on that line, the arrow is free.
  • Removing an arrow never blocks another one, so take every free arrow you see.
  • Start near the edges, where exit lines are short.
  • A bent arrow leaves in the direction of its head, not its tail. Follow the body to the head before you tap.
  • A blocked tap costs 3 seconds on the clock. In calm mode there is no clock, so you can try freely.

Where it appears in the tower

Arrows opens at floor 41, right after camp 40, and that floor is always Arrows. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

This kind of puzzle is called an arrow puzzle or an arrow escape puzzle. In Zirve a blocked arrow costs time rather than a life, and long arrows bend like snakes.
