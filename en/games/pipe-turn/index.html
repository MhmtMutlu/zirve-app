Pipe turn: pipe rotation puzzle

Pipe turn fills the board with pipe pieces: ends, straights, corners and T pieces. Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn and make the water run from the source to the flag; from floor 40, every piece must join the flow.

How to play

Turn the pipes and get the water flowing.

  1. Tap a pipe to turn it 90 degrees.
  2. Lead the water from the source to the flag without a gap; flowing pipes fill up.

Higher up

  • From floor 40: every pipe must be connected
  • More branches, more tangled pipes
  • A 6×6 board from floor 100

Tips

  • Start next to the source and the flag. They never turn, so the pieces beside them have fewer possible directions.
  • Pieces on the edge of the board cannot point outwards. Turn them inwards first.
  • Each tap is a quarter turn clockwise. A straight piece needs at most one tap, any other piece at most three.
  • From floor 40 every piece must join the network: an open end pointing at the wall or at a piece that does not answer means something is still wrong.
  • The count above the board shows how many squares the water reaches. Use it to check your progress.

Where it appears in the tower

Pipe turn opens at floor 21, right after camp 20, and that floor is always Pipe turn. From then on it comes up mixed with the other games, getting harder as you climb.

In calm mode you can play every game you have unlocked with no timer and without spending lives. It gives no progress; it is there for practice.

What this kind of puzzle is called

This type is known as a pipe puzzle, a plumber puzzle or a network puzzle. In Zirve the source and the flag stay fixed and the board never starts solved.
