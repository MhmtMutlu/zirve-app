What do the squares mean?

Each square of a shared result is one floor: top row 1–5, bottom row 6–10. Every square has its own colour and icon, so it reads without colour too.

  • ⭐ At 3-star speed

    Solved with at least 15 seconds left.

  • 🟩 Solved

    Solved before the time ran out.

  • 🟨 With a continue

    Time ran out; solved after continuing with an ad or gems.

  • 🟥 Time ran out

    The attempt ended on this floor.

  • ⬜ Not reached

    The attempt did not get this far.

A 🕒 With time assist line means Time assist was on during the attempt. The squares still follow the normal time; assisted attempts are not ranked.

How to play

Ten floors, three attempts, one ranking.

1

Open the tower

In Zirve, tap the Daily tower card on the tower screen. It renews every midnight and is the same for everyone.

2

Climb

Every floor is a quick puzzle. You get three attempts; the best one is ranked in your country and among friends. Gems at floors 3, 6 and 10.

3

Share

When the attempt is over, tap Share result: the squares and your time go out, the games and boards don't.
