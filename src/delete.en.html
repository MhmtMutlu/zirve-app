Delete in the app

  1. Open Zirve and tap the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings button at the top right, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This works for guest accounts and for accounts signed in with Apple. An internet connection is required.

What is deleted

  • Your account and identity (guest ID or Sign in with Apple link)
  • Your display name, outfit and theme choices, invite code
  • Floor progress, floor results, streak, season points and rewards, Tower of the Day results
  • Gem balance, gem history, inventory and unused gems (not refundable)
  • League entries, friendships, notification records and your device push token
  • Progress and settings on your device (the app is reset)

Deletion is permanent and immediate; it leaves system backups within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and our payment provider RevenueCat for the periods required by law; your store receipts stay in your Apple account. Purchases such as season premium and gems are lost with the deleted account.

If you cannot open the app

If you already removed the app, your guest account remains on our servers. To have it deleted, email {{CONTACT}} with the subject “Delete my account” and include one of the following so we can find it:

  • Your invite code (in the app on the Friends screen: “Your invite code: …”), or
  • If you signed in with Apple, the email address your Apple account shared with us.

After verifying the request we complete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
