This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the Zirve mobile game (iPhone and iPad) and this website (the “Service”).

Controller: Mehmet Mutlu (independent developer; “Zirve”, “we”). Contact: {{CONTACT}}. Users in Türkiye can also read the KVKK information notice (Turkish).

Summary

  • You do not need to give us a name, email address or phone number to play. On first launch a guest account with a random identifier is created for you.
  • We only process what the game needs to work: your progress, results, gems, league and friend data.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not ask for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not access it.
  • We use no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account in the app with one tap; your game data on our servers is deleted immediately.

3. Visible to other players

  • Players in your league group: your display name, outfit and tower theme, floors cleared this week and your rank.
  • Your friends: your display name, outfit, record floor, league and when you last played.
  • Tower of the Day leaderboard: your display name, floors cleared and time.

You do not have to use your real name as your display name; inappropriate names may be changed.

5. Ads

Ads in Zirve appear only when you ask for one: when you tap “Watch an ad” to get a life or to continue after time runs out. Nothing interrupts play.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google processes device and app information, IP address (including approximate location derived from it) and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • The app does not ask for iOS “Allow Apps to Request to Track” permission, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (IAB TCF) is shown before any ad loads; personalised ads are shown only with your consent. You can change your choice under privacy choices in the app's Settings.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep game data for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account), your account and all game data linked to it are deleted from our servers immediately and leave system backups within 30 days. See Delete your account.
  • Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat for the periods required by law; your store receipts stay in your Apple account.
  • Our provider's security logs (IP address, request time) are kept briefly and expire automatically.
  • Deleting the app only removes data on your device. To delete your guest account on our servers, use “Delete account” in the app first, or email us with your invite code.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS. In the database each player can only access their own data (row-level security); sensitive operations such as balances and rewards run on the server only. No system is perfectly secure; in the event of a breach we will make the notifications required by law.

8. Children

Zirve is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account; contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EEA/UK) and KVKK (Türkiye) you have the right to access your data and receive a copy, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format and withdraw consent. You can delete your account in the app and change your display name there. For other requests email {{CONTACT}}, ideally including your invite code (shown on the Friends screen). We answer within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
