This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the Zirve mobile game (iPhone and iPad) and this website (the “Service”).

Controller: Mehmet Mutlu (independent developer; “Zirve”, “we”). Contact: {{CONTACT}}. Users in Türkiye can also read the KVKK information notice (Turkish).

Summary

  • You don't need to give us a name, email or phone number to play. On first launch a guest account with a random ID is created for you.
  • We process the data the game needs to work: your progress, results, gems, league and friends.
  • We don't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We don't ask for, or access, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We use no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools and we do not sell your data. To improve the game we keep usage statistics for 90 days in our own database (Supabase, EU/Frankfurt), linked to your account but not containing your name or contact details. In the European Economic Area we only do this if you allow it; elsewhere you can turn it off at any time under Profile → Settings → Usage statistics. Turning it off also deletes your past statistics.
  • We keep as little as possible: we don't store your IP address in our database, we don't ask Apple for your name or email when you sign in, and we keep data only as long as its purpose needs (Retention).
  • You can delete your account and download a copy of your data in the app.

3. Usage statistics

  • Statistics are kept only in our own database (Supabase, EU/Frankfurt), and the records linked to your account are deleted after 90 days (in the long run only daily totals not linked to anyone remain); they are never shared with third parties, never used for ads or tracking, and never used to follow you across other apps or websites.
  • European Economic Area (EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway): after the tutorial we ask once, “Share / Don't share”; nothing is recorded until you answer, and nothing if you choose “Don't share”. We tell your region from your App Store country and from what the ad consent form reports; if it can't be told, we treat you as being in a region where we ask.
  • Other countries (Türkiye, the UK, Switzerland and elsewhere): statistics are on by default; we tell you so in one line on first launch.
  • Everywhere you can switch them off or on under Profile → Settings → Usage statistics. Turning them off stops recording and deletes your past statistics from our server. We keep a record of your choices (when, which text version) for as long as your account exists, to prove consent.

4. Visible to other players

  • Players in your league group: your display name, outfit and tower theme, floors climbed this week and rank.
  • Your friends: your display name, outfit, record floor, league and when you last played.
  • Daily Tower ranking: your display name, floors cleared and time.
  • Weekly calm list: your display name, outfit and the number of puzzles you solved in calm mode this week.

Don't use your real name as display name; pick a nickname. The name screen reminds you; names that look like an email address, phone number or web address are not accepted. Inappropriate names may be changed.

6. Ads

Ads in Zirve appear only when you ask for one: when you tap “Watch ad” to get a life or continue after time runs out. No ad interrupts play, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google processes device and app information, your IP address (including an approximate location derived from it) and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • The app does not ask for iOS “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google's consent form (IAB TCF) is shown before any ad loads; personalised ads are shown only with your consent. You can change your choice in the app's privacy options. This form is not consent to our usage statistics; we ask for those separately.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Retention and deletion

  • We keep game data for as long as your account exists.
  • Usage statistics are kept for at most 90 days and deleted immediately when you turn them off or delete your account. Daily totals not linked to anyone (e.g. players per day) remain for 13 months.
  • Diagnostic reports are kept for 30 days, the notification log for 30 days, the record of floors where time ran out for 90 days, calm-mode solves for 30 days (weekly counts 90 days).
  • Your push token is removed if you don't open the app for 60 days. Sign-in sessions keep no IP address or device details; sign-in security records are kept without IP address and email for at most 1 hour.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account), your account and the game data linked to it are deleted immediately and leave system backups within 30 days. Exception: to audit cheating and refunds, gem movements and cheat flags are kept for at most 12 months and purchase events (product, date, country, currency, price, transaction ID) for at most 24 months, with the link to your account removed; then they are deleted or made anonymous. We also ask our purchase provider RevenueCat to delete its record, and if you signed in with Apple we revoke the app's Sign in with Apple authorization. We record the deletion itself with only a date and a random request number (not your ID) for 3 years. See Delete your account.
  • Your store receipts remain in your Apple account.
  • The infrastructure logs of our hosting provider Supabase (IP address, request time) are kept for a few days and deleted automatically; we neither create nor extend them.
  • Deleting the app only removes data on your device. To delete your guest account on our servers, use “Delete account” in the app first, or email us with the support code shown in the app.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS. In the database each player can only access their own data (row-level security); usage statistics cannot be accessed directly from outside; sensitive operations such as balances and rewards happen only on the server. No system is completely secure; in case of a breach we make the notifications the law requires.

9. Children

Zirve is a puzzle game anyone can play, but it is not designed specifically for children and is not in the App Store Kids category. We do not ask for your name, email or date of birth; ads are only shown on request and limited to content suitable for general audiences. If we learn that a child under 13 (or a higher age where your country requires it) has given us personal data, we will delete the account; please contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EEA/UK) and KVKK (Türkiye) you have the right to access your data and receive a copy, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format and withdraw consent.

Right to object: You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest, in particular usage statistics. The easiest way is to switch off Profile → Settings → Usage statistics; recording stops and your past statistics are deleted. If you gave consent, you can withdraw it in the same place; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

You can get a copy of your data right away under Profile → Settings → Account → Download my data. You can delete your account in the app and change your display name there. For other requests email {{CONTACT}} and include the support code shown in the app (Profile → Settings → Account); it is only visible on your device and lets us verify the request comes from you. We answer within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Our representative in the EU and the UK: the appointment of our representative under Art. 27 GDPR / UK GDPR is in progress; their contact details will be published here once appointed. Until then you can reach us directly at {{CONTACT}}.
